# Archived plans

The plans this folder holds are finished or superseded and are **never edited here**. Every item
still open was carried, text unchanged, into [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) on 2026-09-17; what stays
here is the built items with their `verified:` and `corrected:` notes, the dropped items with the
decisions that dropped them, and each plan's changelog. IDs are the originals, and decisions are
cited by bare number the way each plan wrote them — `PLAN.md` cites the same decisions with the
plan's letter in front.
