## What it is

The first screen after signing in, at `/dashboard`. It is made of **cards**, each one a small box
with one job.

## What is on it

- **The organization's name** as the heading, and "You are … here." with your role.
- **Go to**: links to each area you have permission to use. Areas you cannot use are not shown.
- **Members**: "Members: …", how many members the organization has, if you may see members.
- **Hidden cards**: the cards you have hidden, with a way to bring each one back. It appears only
  once hiding cards is switched on. See [Dashboard cards](/docs/admin-guide/widgets).
- **The bell** in the header, with your unread notifications.
- **The Organization menu** in the header, to switch organizations.

> [!TIP]
> If someone says a card is missing from their dashboard, open **Hidden cards** → **Why is
> something missing?**. It names the reason for every card. See
> [Dashboard cards](/docs/admin-guide/widgets).
